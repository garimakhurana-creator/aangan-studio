# Approved facts the agent may state

Every line here is sourced. The agent may say these and nothing else about the studio. Anything not here: "That's something your designer will go through with you at the consultation."

## The studio
- Aangan Studio does end-to-end interior design for homes and small offices in Pune city and PCMC. (services.md)
- Founded by Nikhil Deshpande. (case brief)
- We design and execute: space planning, materials, furniture (custom and sourced), lighting, kitchens and wardrobes, and we supervise execution with our own network of contractors and vendors. (services.md)
- Our portfolio is on our website and Instagram. (W10, case brief)
- The studio has a team of designers; the studio assigns the designer for each project, and you meet them at the consultation. For large homes the principal designer can come to the site. (case brief: 14 designers; T12)

## What we take on
- Homes: apartments, independent houses, villas. Full home, a full floor, two or more rooms, or a single bedroom or living room as a complete redesign with execution. (services.md)
- Offices, clinics and studios up to about 3,000 square feet. (services.md)
- Rented homes are fine as long as there are no structural changes; kitchens can be modular units that come out cleanly. (T11)
- We incorporate Vastu requirements into our designs. (services.md)

## What we don't do
- Architecture or structural work: we don't move or break walls, change the structure, or handle structural permits. (services.md)
- Advice-only, colour or styling visits; standalone furniture sourcing; standalone Vastu consultation. (services.md)
- Restaurants, hotels, retail stores, gyms. (services.md)
- Projects outside Pune city and PCMC, e.g. Talegaon, Lonavala, Nashik, Mumbai. (services.md)

## Process and timelines
- It starts with a consultation with one of our designers. The consultation is free and there's no obligation. (T02, T13)
- The consultation can be at the studio or a site visit at the caller's home or office. (T05, T12, W01)
- Design takes three to four weeks from the first consultation. (services.md)
- Execution takes roughly eight to sixteen weeks depending on size and how ready the site is. (services.md)
- We can't take on a project that needs to be finished in under six weeks. (services.md)
- Pricing is worked out by the designer at the consultation, based on the site, materials and scope. (pricing.md)

## Numbers the agent is allowed to say
Only these: three to four weeks (design), eight to sixteen weeks (execution), six weeks (minimum), about 3,000 square feet (office limit), 15 minutes (senior callback for an existing client), and the caller's own details read back to them.
